OFF THE RAILS - iPhone setup (one time, about 5 minutes on your laptop)

1. Go to github.com, sign in, click New repository.
   Name it off-the-rails, set it to Public, click Create repository.
2. Click "uploading an existing file". Drag in every file from this folder
   (index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest and the three .png icons). Click Commit changes.
3. In the repo: Settings > Pages. Under "Build and deployment" choose
   Deploy from a branch, branch main, folder / (root). Save.
   After about a minute your link appears: https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/off-the-rails/
4. On your iPhone, open that link in Safari. Tap Share > Add to Home Screen.
5. Open it from the new home-screen icon once while you have signal.
   From then on it works with no signal.

Always open it from the home-screen icon: iPhone keeps that app's progress separate from Safari.
New weeks: ask Claude for the next week pack (.json), save it to Files, then in the app go to Settings > Load week pack.
